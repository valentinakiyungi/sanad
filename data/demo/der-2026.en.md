# Demo Employment Regulation (DER-2026)

> Fictional regulation written to test Sanad. It is NOT UAE law and must not be relied on.

## Article (1) - Definitions
In this Regulation, the following terms have the meanings set out below:
- "Employer": any natural or legal person who employs one or more workers.
- "Worker": any natural person who works for an Employer in return for a Wage.
- "Wage": the basic pay plus any allowances agreed in the employment contract.
- "Probation Period": the trial period at the start of employment.

## Article (2) - Employment Contract
The employment contract shall be written and fixed-term, and its term shall not exceed three years. It may be renewed by agreement of both parties. A copy shall be given to the Worker within 7 days of signing.

## Article (3) - Probation Period
The Probation Period shall not exceed six months. Either party may end the contract during the Probation Period by giving 14 days' written notice, subject to Article (8).

## Article (4) - Working Hours
Maximum ordinary working hours are 8 hours per day or 48 hours per week. During the holy month of Ramadan, daily working hours are reduced by two hours. Overtime is governed by Article (5).

## Article (5) - Overtime
Overtime shall not exceed 2 hours per day. The Worker is entitled to the Wage for the hour plus 25%. Overtime worked between 10 pm and 4 am is paid at the Wage for the hour plus 50%.

## Article (6) - Annual Leave
A Worker who completes one year of service is entitled to 30 calendar days of paid annual leave. A Worker who has served more than six months but less than one year is entitled to 2 days for each month. Unused leave is paid out when employment ends in accordance with Article (8).

## Article (7) - Sick Leave
After the Probation Period, a Worker is entitled to 90 days of sick leave per year: the first 15 days with full Wage, the next 30 days with half Wage, and the remainder unpaid. A medical report is required.

## Article (8) - End of Service
When employment ends, the Employer shall pay all dues within 14 days, including an end-of-service gratuity of 21 days' basic Wage for each of the first five years of service. A Worker whose contract is ended during the Probation Period under Article (3) is not entitled to the gratuity.
