# Demo Consumer Finance Regulation (DCFR-2026)

> Fictional regulation written to test Sanad. It is NOT UAE law and must not be relied on.

## Article (1) - Definitions
In this Regulation, the following terms have the meanings set out below:
- "Licensed Lender": any bank or finance company licensed to grant consumer credit.
- "Consumer": any natural person who obtains credit for personal purposes.
- "Personal Loan": credit granted to a Consumer without collateral.
- "Debt Burden Ratio": total monthly repayments divided by the Consumer's monthly income.

## Article (2) - Disclosure Before Contract
Before signing, the Licensed Lender shall give the Consumer a key facts statement in Arabic and English showing the annual percentage rate, the total cost of credit and all fees. The Consumer has a cooling-off period of 5 business days under Article (6).

## Article (3) - Debt Burden Limit
The Debt Burden Ratio shall not exceed 50% of the Consumer's monthly income, including the new Personal Loan.

## Article (4) - Maximum Loan Amount and Term
A Personal Loan shall not exceed 20 times the Consumer's monthly income, and the repayment term shall not exceed 48 months, subject to the limit in Article (3).

## Article (5) - Early Settlement
The Consumer may settle a loan early, in full or in part. The early settlement fee shall not exceed 1% of the outstanding amount, capped at 10,000 dirhams.

## Article (6) - Cooling-off Period
The Consumer may cancel the credit agreement within 5 business days of signing without penalty, provided the funds are returned. Fees disclosed under Article (2) shall be refunded.

## Article (7) - Late Payment
Late payment fees shall not exceed 200 dirhams per month. The Licensed Lender shall notify the Consumer in writing before reporting a default, and complaints are handled under Article (8).

## Article (8) - Complaints
The Licensed Lender shall acknowledge a complaint within 2 business days and resolve it within 30 days. If the complaint is not resolved, the Consumer may escalate it to the competent authority.
